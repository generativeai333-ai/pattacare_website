Two Sides of Seamless Recovery

Designed for Patients. Engineered for Care Teams.

Post-operative recovery succeeds only when both sides of the care equation benefit. Here is how PattaCare delivers effortless engagement for patients and calm clarity for clinicians.

The Patient Experience

Zero Friction & Peace of Mind

Recovering at home should not require wrestling with app store downloads, forgotten passwords, or complicated web portals.

  • Familiar WhatsApp Interface: Patients reply in seconds within the messaging app they and their families already use every day.
  • 30-Second Daily Check-Ins: Structured prompts tailored to each specific post-op day (POD 1 through POD 30) without fatigue.
  • Continuous Reassurance: Patients feel cared for knowing their surgical team has continuous visibility into their healing trajectory.
  • Family & Caregiver Support: With patient consent, designated family members can receive daily recovery updates.

The Surgical Care Team

Proactive Triage & Time Savings

Clinical nurse coordinators and surgeons gain proactive visibility into their entire post-op cohort without adding administrative burnout.

  • Cohort-Wide Trajectory Visibility: See at a glance which patients are recovering smoothly and which require attention.
  • Early Deviation Detection: Identify surgical site infection signals, pain spikes, or mobility regressions before emergency visits occur.
  • Drastically Reduced Phone Tag: Replaces unstructured routine discharge calls and phone queues with structured data feeds.
  • Auditable Documentation: Automated timestamps and structured patient reports ready for export into hospital EMR charts.
The Care Continuity Loop

How PattaCare Connects the Surgical Recovery Journey

A structured, predictable 4-step loop that transforms fragmented post-discharge recovery into continuous clinical confidence.

01 Check In

Frictionless Daily Reporting

Patients provide a 30-second structured check-in via WhatsApp covering wound appearance, pain score, mobility progress, and cellular device vitals.

02 Organize

Trajectory Structuring

PattaCare structures patient-reported outcomes against expected clinical baselines for their specific surgical procedure and post-op day.

03 Highlight

Early Deviation Detection

Deviations in recovery trajectory — such as localized wound heat, fever, or abrupt pain escalation — are instantly highlighted on the triage board.

04 Act

Clinician Decision Support

Surgical coordinators and surgeons review stratified patients and determine the appropriate next step: calm reassurance, medication review, or clinic follow-up.

Hospital Platform Architecture & Enterprise Security

Interoperability & Data Standards
