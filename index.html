Medication Adherence

Smart Pillbox Telemetry, Missed-Dose Root Cause Triage & Predictive Refills

Medication non-adherence accounts for over 30% of preventable hospital admissions. PattaCare pairs connected pill dispensers with conversational WhatsApp root-cause triage and automated pharmacy resupply.

01

Smart Pillbox & Inhaler Telemetry

Cellular and BLE pill organizers capture opening timestamps automatically. If the dispenser is not opened within a 45-minute window of the scheduled dose, an automated reminder is queued.

02

Conversational Root Cause Triage

When a dose is missed, PattaCare initiates a gentle WhatsApp dialogue to diagnose the reason: side effects (nausea/dizziness), forgotten dose, running out of pills, or cost barriers.

03

Care Coordinator & Family Escalation

Sustained non-adherence (e.g. 2 consecutive doses of oral anticoagulants or insulin) alerts the nurse coordinator and sends a push notification to designated family caregivers.

04

Predictive Auto-Refills & Delivery

Calculates remaining pill inventory based on confirmed doses. Prompts the patient 7 days before depletion and syncs with hospital outpatient pharmacies or delivery partners.

Early Deterioration Alerts

Automated NEWS2 Scoring, Multi-Signal Composite Rules & Alarm Fatigue Defenses

Detect clinical deterioration hours before acute cardiac arrest or emergency ICU transfers. Operates in dual mode: hospital ward continuous step-down monitoring and remote home surveillance.

National Early Warning Score 2 (NEWS2) Engine

PattaCare's rules engine continuously aggregates physiological parameters into a validated composite score:

Respiration Rate (RR) 12–20 / min (0 pts)
Oxygen Saturation (SpO2) ≥ 96% on room air (0 pts)
Systolic Blood Pressure 111–219 mmHg (0 pts)
Pulse Rate (Heart Rate) 51–90 bpm (0 pts)
Body Temperature 36.1–38.0 °C (0 pts)

Enterprise Clinical Defenses

  • Personalized Baselines: Supports clinician overrides for patients with chronic baseline deviations (e.g. COPD baseline SpO2 88-92% mapped to NEWS2 Scale 2).
  • Anti-Alarm Fatigue Protocol: Intelligent de-duplication, alert bundling, and configurable snooze intervals prevent clinician notification burnout.
  • Closed-Loop Timed Escalation: Critical alerts (NEWS2 ≥ 7) require electronic nurse acknowledgement within 5 minutes or escalate to on-call physician and rapid response teams.
  • Ward Mode & Home Mode: Flexible thresholds whether deployed on continuous hospital ward beds (Dozee/VinCense style) or asynchronous home monitoring.
