# Patta Care — Health-Tech Website Design Specification

## 1. Purpose

Create a modern, trustworthy, AI-enabled healthcare website for **Patta Care**.

The design should communicate:

- Healthcare trust
- Human care
- Modern technology
- AI-assisted healthcare
- Telehealth / digital care
- Secure health information
- Continuity of care
- Clinical professionalism
- Accessibility and simplicity

The visual direction should feel closer to a **modern U.S. health-tech company** than to a traditional hospital website.

Use the design philosophy of modern health-tech products such as Abridge, Oscar Health, and Neko Health as inspiration for sophistication, whitespace, clarity, and technology-led healthcare storytelling. Do not copy their branding, layouts, logos, illustrations, or proprietary visual identity.

---

## 2. Brand Positioning

### Brand name

**Patta Care**

### Recommended positioning

**Patta Care — Smarter healthcare, connected around you.**

Alternative supporting message:

**Technology that helps people, doctors, and healthcare teams stay connected.**

The website should position Patta Care as a healthcare technology platform rather than a hospital.

### Brand personality

The visual language should be:

- Trustworthy
- Calm
- Intelligent
- Human
- Modern
- Professional
- Approachable
- Secure
- Premium but not luxurious
- Technology-enabled but not overly futuristic

Avoid making the product look like a generic AI startup.

---

# 3. Core Design Principle

## Clinical Trust + AI Intelligence

The primary visual idea is:

**Healthcare trust**
+
**Human connection**
+
**Technology intelligence**

The site should immediately communicate that Patta Care is serious about healthcare while using modern technology underneath the experience.

The first impression should be:

> “This is a credible healthcare company built with modern technology.”

It should NOT feel like:

- A traditional hospital website
- A pharmaceutical website
- A generic wellness website
- A crypto/Web3 website
- A cybersecurity company
- A futuristic sci-fi AI company

# 4. Color System

Use a restrained healthcare blue + AI teal system.

## Primary

**Deep Healthcare Blue**

`#1B4B91`

Use for:

- Primary buttons
- Navigation highlights
- Major headings where appropriate
- Links
- Important icons
- Brand elements
- Key UI controls

This is the dominant brand color.

## Secondary

**Healthcare / AI Teal**

`#14B8A6`

Use for:

- AI-related elements
- Secondary CTAs
- Feature highlights
- Status indicators
- Data visualization
- Healthcare technology accents
- Small decorative elements

Teal should be an accent, not the dominant page color.

## Dark Text

**Clinical Navy**

`#0B1F33`

Use for:

- Main headings
- Body text where strong contrast is needed
- Navigation
- Footer
- Enterprise/clinical sections

## Main Background

`#F6FAFC`

Use as the primary page background.

## Light Blue Section

`#EAF4FA`

Use for:

- Feature sections
- Healthcare workflow areas
- Testimonials
- Product explanations
- Supporting panels

## Positive / Health Green

`#43B98C`

Use sparingly for:

- Success states
- Positive health indicators
- Completed steps
- Small status indicators

## White

`#FFFFFF`

Use for:

- Cards
- Navigation surfaces
- Hero content areas
- Forms
- Modals
- Clean clinical sections

---

# 5. Color Ratio

Maintain approximately:

- 50–55% white / very light backgrounds
- 25–30% deep blue / navy
- 10–15% teal
- 5% green and other accents

Do not fill the website with blue.

Do not use large neon gradients.

Do not use purple as the primary brand color.

Do not use bright green as the dominant healthcare color.

---

# 6. Gradients

Use gradients only when they materially improve the design.

Preferred:

- Very subtle blue → teal transitions
- Low-contrast background gradients
- Soft radial highlights behind hero content

Avoid:

- Strong rainbow gradients
- Neon AI gradients
- Purple/blue cyberpunk gradients
- Highly saturated backgrounds

The website should remain clinical and trustworthy.

---

# 7. Typography

Use a modern, highly readable sans-serif font.

Preferred options:

### Primary option

**Inter**

Weights:

- 400 Regular
- 500 Medium
- 600 SemiBold
- 700 Bold

### Alternative

**Manrope**

Use if a slightly more contemporary/premium appearance is desired.

Typography must prioritize readability over visual novelty.

---

# 8. Typography Scale

Desktop:

- Hero heading: 56–72px
- Hero supporting text: 20–22px
- H1: 48–56px
- H2: 36–44px
- H3: 24–30px
- Body: 16–18px
- Small text: 13–14px
- Button text: 15–16px

Mobile:

- Hero heading: 38–46px
- H1: 34–40px
- H2: 28–34px
- H3: 22–26px
- Body: 16px

Use generous line-height.

Do not create dense blocks of text.

---

# 9. Overall Layout

Use a modern SaaS/health-tech layout.

Recommended maximum content width:

**1200–1280px**

Use:

- Large whitespace
- Clear vertical rhythm
- Generous section spacing
- Rounded cards
- Subtle borders
- Minimal shadows

Suggested border radius:

- Small: 8px
- Medium: 12px
- Large: 18–24px

Avoid excessive pill-shaped components.

---

The hero should immediately explain what Patta Care does.

Suggested headline:

**Healthcare, connected around you.**

Supporting text:

**Patta Care brings people, providers, health information, and intelligent technology together to make healthcare simpler and more connected.**

Primary CTA:

**Get Started**

Secondary CTA:

**See How It Works**

Hero should use:

- Large typography
- White/light background
- Deep blue primary text
- Teal AI accent
- Human healthcare imagery OR sophisticated health-data visualization

Avoid a generic doctor stock photograph occupying the entire hero.

---

# 12. Hero Visual Direction

Preferred visual concept:

A clean digital healthcare ecosystem.

Show a subtle visual relationship between:

**Patient → AI → Doctor → Health Record → Care**

Use:

- Patient profile
- Health timeline
- Secure health record
- Doctor consultation
- AI assistant
- Connected care indicators

Visual style:

- Minimal
- Premium
- Semi-realistic
- Soft 3D or high-quality UI visualization
- Blue/teal accents

Do not make it look like science fiction.

---

# 13. Trust Section

Immediately after the hero, establish credibility.

Possible heading:

**Healthcare technology designed around people.**

Show 3–4 trust principles:

### Human-first

Technology supports people and healthcare professionals.

### Connected

Relevant health information stays connected across the care journey.

### Intelligent

AI helps organize information and reduce administrative friction.

### Secure

Privacy and security are fundamental to the platform.

Use simple line icons.

Avoid giant badges and excessive certifications unless real certifications are available.

Never invent compliance claims.

---

# 14. Platform Section

Heading:

**One connected healthcare experience.**

Create cards for:

### AI Health Assistant

Help users understand and navigate their healthcare journey.

### Telehealth

Connect patients with healthcare professionals remotely.

### Health Profile

Keep important health information organized and accessible.

### Medical Records

Bring relevant health records into one connected experience.

### Care Coordination

Help patients and providers stay aligned.

### Intelligent Workflows

Use AI and automation to reduce repetitive healthcare tasks.

Each card should include:

- Small icon
- Short heading
- 1–2 sentence explanation
- Optional “Learn more”

---

# 15. AI Section

This is an important differentiator.

Use a distinct but restrained teal/blue section.

Heading:

**AI that works with healthcare — not instead of it.**

Supporting message:

**Patta Care uses intelligent technology to organize information, simplify workflows, and support better-connected care while keeping people and healthcare professionals at the center.**

Visual:

Patient data → AI intelligence → clinician → action

Use animated micro-interactions if supported.

Avoid claims such as:

- “AI diagnoses everything”
- “AI replaces doctors”
- “Guaranteed diagnosis”
- “Guaranteed treatment”

AI should be presented as an assistant/support layer unless the actual product has validated clinical capabilities.

---

# 16. Telehealth Section

Heading:

**Care when and where you need it.**

Visual:

- Patient on mobile/laptop
- Doctor consultation
- Appointment information
- Secure connection indicator

CTA:

**Connect With Care**

Use blue as the primary CTA and teal for supporting elements.

---

# 17. Provider Section

The provider experience should feel more enterprise-oriented.

Heading:

**Give healthcare teams better context.**

Show workflow:

1. Patient shares information
2. Patta Care organizes the context
3. AI prepares useful insights
4. Provider reviews
5. Provider delivers care
6. Information becomes part of the longitudinal record

Use a horizontal workflow on desktop and vertical timeline on mobile.

---

# 18. Patient Journey

Create a clear 5-step journey:

**01 — Tell us what you need**

**02 — Build your health context**

**03 — Connect with care**

**04 — Receive personalized support**

**05 — Continue your healthcare journey**

Use numbered cards with blue and teal accents.

---

# 19. Data / Interoperability Visual

If the product supports healthcare interoperability, show it visually.

Concept:

**Patient**
↓
**Patta Care**
↓
**Health Data**
↓
**FHIR / APIs**
↓
**Providers / Healthcare Systems**

Technical interoperability can be highlighted on the enterprise/platform page.

---

# 20. Security Section

Heading:

**Built with privacy and security in mind.**

Use 3–4 cards:

- Secure data handling
- Privacy-focused architecture
- Controlled access
- Auditable workflows

Only use specific compliance statements such as HIPAA compliant/HIPAA certified if they are factually applicable.

Never create fake security badges.

---

- Rounded corners
- Small quotation

- Role

# 25. Cards

Cards should have:

- White background
- 1px subtle border
- 12–20px radius
- Very light shadow or no shadow
- Comfortable padding

Preferred style:

`border: 1px solid #E2EAF0`

Avoid:

- Heavy shadows
- Glassmorphism everywhere
- Excessive borders
- Extremely rounded cartoon-like cards

---

# 26. Buttons

- Radius: 8–10px
- Medium/heavy weight

- White/light background
- Blue border
- Blue text

AI/technology CTA:

- Teal can be used selectively

# 29. AI Visual Language

AI should appear through:

- Connected nodes
- Data flows
- Health timelines
- Intelligent summaries
- Context cards
- Soft teal highlights
- Subtle animated particles

Do not use:

- Robot heads
- Humanoid robots
- Neon brain graphics
- Sci-fi holograms everywhere
- Cyberpunk aesthetics

The AI should feel **embedded into healthcare**, not like a separate robot product.

---

# 30. Motion

Use subtle motion.

Recommended:

- Fade-in
- Slide-up
- Gentle card movement
- Data-flow animation
- Number transitions
- Hover elevation
- Soft background movement

Animation duration:

**200–500ms**

Avoid:

- Excessive parallax
- Fast flashing animations
- Large page transitions
- Distracting particle effects

Accessibility:

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

---

# 31. Responsive Design

Desktop:

- 1440px optimized
- 1200–1280px content container

Tablet:

- 768–1024px

Mobile:

- 375–430px

Mobile requirements:

- Sticky compact navigation
- Hamburger menu
- Full-width primary CTA where appropriate
- Cards stack vertically
- Hero typography reduced
- No horizontal scrolling
- Touch targets at least approximately 44px
- Maintain strong readability

---

# 32. Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA where practical.

Requirements:

- Strong color contrast
- Keyboard navigation
- Visible focus states
- Accessible form labels
- Alt text for meaningful images
- Semantic HTML
- Avoid color-only status indicators
- Support reduced motion
- Clear error messages
- Large enough touch targets

Never rely solely on teal/green/red to communicate medical status.

---

# 33. Forms

Healthcare forms should feel calm and simple.

Use:

- Clear labels
- Short fields
- Progressive disclosure
- Helpful validation
- Clear error messages
- Privacy reassurance near sensitive fields

Example:

**Tell us what you need help with**

[ Describe your concern ]

[ Continue ]

Avoid asking for unnecessary health information early in the journey.

# 35. Provider Dashboard Visual Language

Provider dashboard should be more information-dense than the patient UI.

Suggested areas:

- Today's appointments
- Patient summary
- AI-generated pre-visit context
- Clinical notes
- Records
- Tasks

- Care timeline

**Navy + blue + neutral gray**
