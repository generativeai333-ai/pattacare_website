## 1. Brand & Core Mission
- **Brand Name:** PattaCare
- **Tagline:** "Care that continues after discharge."
- **Core Mission:** Eliminating the critical 30-day post-discharge clinical blind spot for hospitals, health systems, and surgical departments worldwide. While surgeries are performed with high clinical excellence, over 90% of patient complications, surgical site infections (SSIs), medication deviations, and preventable readmissions happen at home after discharge.
- **Primary Domain:** Post-Operative Recovery Continuity, Outpatient Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM), and Chronic Disease Titration globally.
- **Key Metrics:**
  - **-35% Reduction in 30-Day Hospital Readmissions**
  - **85%+ Patient Daily WhatsApp Response Rate** (vs. <15% for standalone hospital mobile apps)
  - **4x Increase in Nurse & Care Coordinator Patient Management Capacity**

## 2. Core Technical Architecture & Stack
- **Frontend / Portal:** Next.js (TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Responsive Clinician & Coordinator Dashboards)
- **Backend API & Microservices:** Python (FastAPI), Node.js, asynchronous task queues
- **EHR & Interoperability Engine:** Medplum / HL7 FHIR R4 standard data architecture (Epic, Cerner, and Open EHR connectors)
- **Conversational Channel:** Meta WhatsApp Cloud API (WhatsApp Business Platform) for zero-app patient check-ins
- **Telemetry Ingestion:** Cellular (4G/LTE CAT-M1), Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), Wi-Fi, and continuous sensor normalization
- **Clinical Summaries:** Deterministic NEWS2 risk stratification engine + SBAR AI Clinical Summarization Agent (Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation)
- **Healthcare Regulatory & Privacy Infrastructure:** HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) ready, GDPR compliant, Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM / ABHA ID linking), and DPDP Act 2023 certified privacy architecture

## 3. Key Product Features & Capabilities
1. **Zero-App Patient Engagement:**
   - Recovering surgical patients and elderly individuals worldwide suffer from severe app fatigue (88% drop-off within 14 days). PattaCare operates 100% natively on WhatsApp without requiring app downloads, new logins, or passwords.
2. **Deterministic Triage (Zero Hallucination):**
   - Clinical answers are triaged through strictly defined surgical department protocols (Routine Green, Review Amber, Urgent Red). AI never makes unauthorized diagnostic decisions; licensed clinicians retain complete medical authority.
3. **Automated SBAR Clinical Handovers:**
   - Triaged patient data, wound observations, and telemetry anomalies are synthesized by an SBAR AI Agent into standardized medical handover notes, allowing surgeons to review and sign off in under 30 seconds.
4. **Plug-and-Play Cellular Medical Devices:**
   - Blood pressure monitors, continuous pulse oximeters, blood glucose/CGM devices, digital scales, and smart medication pillboxes with zero home Wi-Fi pairing required.

## 4. Market & Competitor Positioning (Complementary to In-Ward Monitors e.g., Dozee)
- **In-Ward Virtual ICUs (e.g., Dozee / dozee.health):**
   - In-ward systems provide contactless under-mattress ballistocardiography (BCG) sensor sheets for in-hospital ward beds and step-down units.
   - **Boundary:** Hardware remains under the hospital mattress; clinical oversight ends the moment the patient walks out the hospital discharge doors.
- **Post-Discharge Home Care (PattaCare):**
   - **"Where In-Ward Monitoring Ends at Discharge, PattaCare Begins."** PattaCare extends clinical oversight into the patient's home for 30–90 days using WhatsApp, cellular telemetry, and SBAR reporting.
   - **Relationship:** PattaCare is strictly **complementary** to in-ward solutions, providing the outpatient bridge that Chief Medical Officers need to satisfy Joint Commission / NABH clinical quality mandates, improve CMS Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program (HRRP) benchmarks, and eliminate preventable 30-day readmissions.
